# Surface brief: Swasthya landing page (signed out)

Mode: Persuade. The visitor decides whether to start a rehabilitation plan (patient) or to prescribe through Swasthya (therapist). Real product truth only; no fabricated statistics, testimonials, logos, certifications or outcomes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The same physio's handout world as the app, spent on one page: the hero artifact is a real exercise sheet (numbered exercises, pen-ticked sets x reps, a therapist's handwritten note), then the product's own screens drawn from the real components, labelled as examples. The sheet is used selectively; the rest of the page is a calm, precise, mostly white healthcare product page, not a paper costume.

STORY: Your therapist writes the plan. You follow it at home. The camera helps you do it correctly. Your therapist sees how it went and changes it. The therapist stays the authority.

FIRST VIEWPORT (desktop 1440x900): top bar with wordmark, in-page links (Product, For patients, For therapists, How it works), Sign in, Get started. Left: the promise as the heading, two sentences of plain copy, Get started + I already have an account, one line that it does not diagnose. Right: the exercise sheet, ruled, with two numbered exercises, ticked boxes, a handwritten note. Phone: heading, copy, CTAs, then the sheet; menu collapses.

REFUSES: card grids of icon + heading + text, hero-metric strips, eyebrow labels, gradient text, glass, stock imagery, device mockups, fake numbers presented as real, "AI-powered" language.

TRUTH RULES: every product claim must be something the app does today. Example screens are labelled as examples with made-up values. No claim of manual (camera-free) logging, HIPAA, clinical outcomes, ratings, user counts.

MOTION: one authored moment only: the ticks on the hero sheet draw on, staggered, once. Respects reduced motion (already global).

FORM: Own list position 1 inherited from the app direction; Persuade scale (display up to 3.5rem, body 17-18px, 68ch measure).
