# Heel Raise

> **Awaiting physiotherapist review.** Every number below is an engineering default, tuned on synthetic geometry and not on real footage. It is not clinical advice.

Template `heel-raise` v1 · Ankle and calf · Lower Body · Beginner

Rise up onto your toes, pause, and lower your heels slowly. Strengthens the calf and helps balance.

## Camera and start position
- **View:** either. From the front or side, about 2 m away, so both legs and feet are fully in frame.
- **Joints that must be visible:** hip, knee, ankle, heel, foot (also used if visible: shoulder).
- **Start position:** Stand tall with your feet flat, holding a steady support. Held for 1.2 s before any rep is judged.
- *Why:* The starting position is held for about a second so the person's own resting position is captured before any rep is judged.

## A rep is GOOD (and only then counted) when ALL of these are true
1. **It reaches the minimum range:** the lift rises to 15% (starting from about 5%). *Why:* A good rep is one where the heel lifts 15% of the shin length. Engineering default, not tuned on real footage.
2. **It returns to the start zone** (5%) before it ends. *Why:* A rep ends only when the movement returns to the starting zone, so a return is always required; a very slow return is coached, not rejected.
3. **Hold:** it holds at the top for at least 0.4 s (0.5 s with 20% tolerance); any hold the therapist prescribes is also required. *Why:* A hold is mandatory only when one is required: the template's own, or the hold the therapist prescribed, with 20% tolerance so a slight early release is not punished.
4. **It is not flicked through:** the whole cycle takes at least 1 s. *Why:* A whole cycle faster than 1 seconds is flicked through, not performed. This is half of the coaching pace and the only new mandatory tempo rule.
5. **None of these mandatory rules is broken:**
   - **Knees bending during the raise** (`knee_bent`, moderate): the knee measure must stay at least 160° while out / peak / back. Held for 0.6 s before it shows; clears after 0.3 s. *Why:* Bending the knees turns a calf raise into a squat; under 160 degrees is clearly bent.
6. **It was seen well enough to judge:** no more than 1 s of the cycle can be unseen, and every mandatory rule above must be measurable. *Why:* If the camera cannot see the movement for more than a second of a cycle (a third of a second when the rep fell short, so a missed peak is never blamed on the person), or cannot check a mandatory rule, the cycle is 'not seen', never wrong.

## What does NOT count
- **Short** (not counted, "a little farther"): the movement leaves the start clearly but stops below 15%. The band from 10% to 15% is the "almost there" band. *Why:* The old halfway-credit line is now only the 'almost there' band, so the wording can say a little farther. That line is at 10%: past it the raise is nearly there.
- **Broke a rule** (not counted, the rule is named): any mandatory rule above, a missed hold, or a cycle faster than 1 s.
- **Not seen** (not counted, never called wrong): the camera could not see enough to judge. The patient is asked to adjust the camera, not the movement.

## Coaching only (never stops a rep counting)
- **Pace:** a cycle faster than 2 s is coached ("slow down"); one slower than 14 s is not penalised.
- **Return:** if the return stalls for 3.5 s, the patient is reminded to finish returning.
- **Leaning forward** (`trunk_lean`, moderate): the trunk measure must stay at most 14° while out / peak / back. Held for 0.6 s before it shows; clears after 0.3 s. *Why:* Leaning for balance; it only coaches because it is a safety support, not a fault.

## What the patient sees and hears
| Situation | Shown on screen (≤ 24 characters) | Spoken / captioned (first wording) |
|---|---|---|
| insufficient_rise (below) | Lift your heels higher | Rise a little higher onto your toes. |
| too_fast (below) | Slow down | Slow down. Take about two seconds to rise onto your toes. |
| short_hold (below) | Hold at the top | Pause for a moment on your toes before you lower your heels. |
| incomplete_return (above) | Lower your heels fully | Lower your heels all the way to the floor before the next raise. |
| knee_bent (below) | Keep your knees straight | Keep your knees straight as you rise onto your toes. |
| trunk_lean (above) | Stand tall | You are leaning. Stand tall with your shoulders over your hips. |
